Validation Checklist

A portable rule set that checks operational records before they feed any indicator — so decisions rest on data that is complete, unique, and coherent. Each rule reports its finding with a severity; nothing is silently dropped.

Domain-agnostic rules — the same approach used in production data pipelines, here for personnel, equipment and work-order records.

Rules

Principles

  • Report, don't silently filter. Every finding is published with its rule and severity; what to do with it stays explicit.
  • Separate source problems from owner problems. A field missing for many records at once is a source issue, not an operator's fault.
  • Check specifications against the data. When documentation and data disagree, detect the truth from the data and flag it.
  • Keep an exception trail. Validation runs before records feed indicators, and the exceptions remain visible for review.

This mirrors the asset-register and model-validation approach (IRAM-ISO 19650 style: requirements → checks → findings → a decision on the delivery) used in the Vaca Muerta Analytics project, generalized here to any operational dataset.
